# Market research: Dutch ZZP bookkeeping, October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-11. Web research only; no code changes.

Verification legend: **[V]** = stated on a vendor page or a news article that quotes vendor pages; **[S]** = secondary/aggregator/affiliate source (treat as indicative); **[U]** = could not verify.

Important caveats up front:
- Several sources are vendor-sponsored or affiliate content (Finom, Freenance, Subger, Knab, Rompslomp's own blog). Their rankings are not independent.
- The official Moneybird pricing page redirected and could not be fetched in full. Moneybird prices below come from third-party sources that disagree with each other.
- No Reddit threads and no Tweakers forum threads surfaced in search. The user-sentiment section relies mainly on Trustpilot, Capterra and an older Freelance.nl article. Reddit and Tweakers were not verified.
- No Consumentenbond or Kassa test of ZZP bookkeeping software was found [U].

### Newer / niche entrants noticed
- **Freenance** (the publisher of one comparison page) is itself a personal-finance tracker, not a bookkeeping product [S].
- **MoneyMonk**, **Gekko**, **Knab** and **Myfinance** (one App Store review, rating 2.0) appear as cheap or free alternatives [V/S].
- **ZZP-Next** (InformerOnline-linked iOS app) appears in App Store results [U for quality].

### Headline features and paywall patterns
- **Moneybird** [V Moneybird feature list via Freenance, S]: UBL 2.1 / Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 "one click per invoice", OSS, btw-aangifte via Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk, PSD2 reconciliation with major Dutch banks, payment links (Mollie, Stripe, Tikkie). Behind the paywall: bank connection (5 transactions/mo on the cheapest tier), more users and more bank transactions.
- **e-Boekhouden.nl Pro** [S]: UBL export, Peppol via add-on; OSS, btw, PSD2, recurring invoices; multi-currency "limited"; payment links via Mollie and PayPal.
- **Exact Online / SnelStart** [S]: Peppol/UBL, OSS, btw, PSD2, multi-currency, recurring invoices; payment links via Mollie/Stripe.
- **Rompslomp**: free tier monetised by per-invoice fee from 1 Nov 2026 (noted above). Bank link is not in the free tier [V nieuws.nl].
- **Jortt**: free tier is invoices and quotes only; bank connection and bookkeeping are paid [V].
- **MoneyMonk**: hours and mileage registration (urencriterium support), free accountant access [S Freelance.nl 2021].

**Paywall pattern across the market:** free tiers nearly always exclude automatic bank connection. The nieuws.nl article states: "Geen van de gratis pakketten bevat een automatische bankkoppeling" [V]. This is the main lever vendors use.

## 2. What users praise and complain about

### Moneybird (Trustpilot, Q3 2026 snapshot)
- Trustpilot (NL) TrustScore **2.3/5 from 247 reviews**, 34% one-star, 43% five-star; 89 reviews in last 12 months [V, fetched 2026-10]. Source: https://nl.trustpilot.com/review/moneybird.nl
- Complaints (Dutch quotes, verbatim as summarised by the fetch):
  - Price: "Krankzinnig dat er niet een goedkopere optie is voor kleinere boekhoudingen" (1 star).
  - Bank connection removed from cheaper plan: "voor mij is de laatste update met het verdwijnen van bankkoppeling met ing de hamerklap om te stoppen met moneybird." (1 star).
  - Support: "Bij problemen krijg je een slecht werkende chatbot." (1 star). Also: no phone support, chatbot blocks staff.
  - Stability at tax deadlines: "Op de belangrijkste momenten niet stabiel meer. (Tijdens belastingaangiftes)" (1 star).
- Praise: "Wat mij het meest opvalt, is dat onze klanten er zonder uitleg mee aan de slag kunnen." (5 star). Automatic bank transactions and fast purchase-invoice processing also praised.
- Capterra: users cite intuitiveness and price; "takes some time to get to know", some settings hard to find [V/S, Capterra page].
- Moneybird's replies to complaints cite a first package overhaul since 2008 and a 12-month transition discount [V via Trustpilot summary].

### e-Boekhouden.nl
- Praise: dashboard of costs and revenue at a glance; receipts app; 15 months free for starters [S Freelance.nl 2021, Onderneming.nl].
- Complaints: "de functionaliteit niet volledig afgestemd op de wensen van freelancers" (generic for several business types) [S]. 240 bookings/yr cap on zzp plan [S].

### Jortt
- Praise: builds bookkeeping from bank transactions, suits users without bookkeeping knowledge [S Freelance.nl 2021]. Won "belangrijkste ICT-prijs Nederland" per Emerce headline [U detail].
- Complaints: no hours, project or mileage tracking; limited invoice customisation [S Freelance.nl 2021, dated].

### Rompslomp
- Praise (from vendor-adjacent blogs): easy to use, "zonder lastige boekhoudkundige termen", "perfect" for zzp'ers [S, vendor-affiliated].
- Complaints: none verified from independent sources in this search. The per-invoice pricing change (Nov 2026) is likely to generate complaints; not verified in reviews yet [U].

### MoneyMonk
- Praise: "zeer gebruiksvriendelijk", ideal for service providers without accounting background; hour and mileage registration [S Freelance.nl, blog].

### Informer
- Praise: two recognised quality marks, around since 1993 [S Freelance.nl 2021].
- Complaint: "een enorm aanbod aan functies die je waarschijnlijk niet nodig hebt"; may be too complex for starters [S].

### Recurring patterns across sources
1. **Price changes and removed features** on cheaper tiers cause the most anger (Moneybird, Rompslomp, Jortt).
2. **Bank connection** gated behind paid tiers, and occasionally silently dropped.
3. **Support quality** (chatbots, no phone) and **stability during tax deadlines**.
4. **Usability for non-accountants** is the main praise. Clients invoiced or paid without explanation.
5. **Feature overload** on incumbent products (Informer, Exact) versus **missing features** on cheap products (Jortt: no hours/mileage).
6. Freelancers want **urencriterium and mileage** tracking, which MoneyMonk and some others advertise.
7. A 2018 ZZP Barometer / MoneyMonk survey of 1,020 zzp'ers found 77.8% rated **easy btw-aangifte** as important [S, dated 2018].

### Regulatory context
- **Netherlands has no B2B e-invoicing mandate in 2026.** Draft legislation for consultation is planned for Q4 2026; adoption by mid-2028 at the latest; start date 1 Jan 2030 for domestic B2B as recommended by EY [V KPMG TaxNewsFlash May 2026, EY report via accp.econnect.eu]. Source: https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/05/tnf-netherlands-proposed-e-invoicing-and-digital-reporting-framework-under-vida.html
- **Belgium** mandated Peppol e-invoicing for B2B from 1 Jan 2026 [V EY Global Tax News]. Relevant only if the user targets Belgian ZZP'ers.
- **Dutch Peppol format:** NLCIUS and Peppol BIS 3.0, UBL-based, aligned to EN 16931 [S Wolters Kluwer].
- **B2G e-invoicing** has been required for central government since 2017 [S].
- Digipoort / SBR filing rules for ZZP btw were not verified [U].

## 4. UX patterns that make mobile bookkeeping feel good

Sources for these are mostly general product and review evidence; specific competitor UX was only partially verified. Items marked [U] are common patterns in the category, not verified in this research.

1. **Onboarding that asks for the bank first.** Moneybird's "clients can use it without explanation" praise suggests that simple initial setup wins. Bank link during onboarding, with categorisation running on the first import [V/S].
2. **Free-trial without card.** Moneybird uses a 60-day trial with no card; Exact 30 days; SnelStart 60 days [S Freenance].
3. **Inbox for unmatched items.** Automatic bank matching with a review queue for leftovers is implied by Moneybird's "automatic bank transactions and fast processing of purchase invoices" praise [V Trustpilot summary].
4. **Receipt capture by photo with on-device OCR, plus email-in address.** e-Boekhouden's receipt app is a named praise point [S]. Email-forwarding of receipts is commonly offered [U specifics].
5. **Share-sheet import** of PDFs and photos into the app: not verified for any Dutch vendor [U].
6. **Swipe to categorise / confirm:** not verified for any Dutch vendor [U]. This is a common pattern for inbox-style apps; test on device.
7. **Plain language.** Rompslomp and MoneyMonk are praised for avoiding accounting jargon [S]. Informer and Exact are criticised for feature overload [S].
8. **Stable on deadline days.** Complaints about instability at btw/IB deadlines (Moneybird) show that reliability on the 1st-of-quarter and 1st-of-May is a UX requirement [V Trustpilot].
9. **Visible fee.** Vendors that hide the per-invoice or per-transaction cap until it bites (Rompslomp per-invoice fee, Moneybird 5-transaction limit) generate the most anger. Showing usage counters clearly is a differentiator [V/S].

## 5. Prioritised list: "To beat the market for a single ZZP'er, this app should add/improve X"

Realism notes for a free, self-hosted app with no paid APIs:
- **PSD2 bank feeds:** a direct licence is out of reach for a free project. Options:
  - **GoCardless Bank Account Data (formerly Nordigen):** status is contested. GoCardless's product page still lists a Free plan of 50 requisitions/month, but several 2025-2026 community sources (Actual Budget docs, a 2026 self-hosting blog) say new free sign-ups stopped in July 2025 [V page, S community; unresolved]. Verify in the GoCardless portal before relying on it. Per the developer docs, up to 24 months history and 90 days continuous access; some banks rate-limit to 4 calls/day per account [V developer docs].
  - **File import (CAMT / CSV / MT940)** from banks remains the realistic free path. The app already does this.
  - **Manual or PSD2 token-based imports** via user's own bank API keys: complex, varies per bank, high support cost [U].
- **Peppol access point:** requires an accredited access point or a provider, which usually costs money. Generating UBL is free; transmitting via Peppol likely is not [U].
- **Digipoort / SBR:** requires Logius/Belastingdienst certification and a software provider registration. Not realistic in a free project without that certification [U].
- **Payment links:** iDEAL via a PSP (Mollie, Stripe) is possible with the user's own merchant account. Tikkie is ABN AMRO-specific and has no public API for third-party products [U]. Showing a link or QR code to the user's own PSP is realistic.

### Must-have (table stakes to be taken seriously)
1. **Reliable bank import with auto-matching to invoices and expenses.** Every paid tier sells this; the free tier's absence is the market's main pain. The app already has CAMT/CSV import. Add: a one-tap "import from bank app export" flow and a clear, stable matching inbox. Reason: this is the top paywall and top complaint trigger.
2. **Btw-aangifte figures plus a clear export that maps to the Belastingdienst rubrieken** (already present). Add a "review before submit" view that shows each rubriek with source transactions. Reason: deadline-day stability is a named complaint; trust in the numbers is the product.
3. **Zero-friction onboarding for a non-accountant.** A first-run flow that asks only for bank file, business KvK/btw details and invoice template. Reason: "clients can use it without explanation" is the most-praised trait.
4. **Transparent limits.** No hidden caps; show counts per month. Reason: Moneybird and Rompslomp fee changes drive anger.
5. **Sales invoices with payment instructions (IBAN + reference) and reminders** (already partly present). Reason: standard in every product; reminders are included in most paid plans.

### Strong differentiators (where a free self-hosted app can win)
6. **Urencriterium and kilometre tracking in one place.** MoneyMonk leads on this; Jortt lacks it. The app already has hours and trips. Make it the default flow for zelfstandigenaftrek evidence (1,225 hours threshold is widely cited, but verify the current threshold before shipping). Reason: freelancer-specific need that generalist apps neglect.
7. **Receipt capture on device with OCR and an email-in address.** e-Boekhouden's receipt app is praised; a self-hosted email-in address solves the "forward to bookkeeper" pattern with no vendor lock-in. Reason: privacy-minded users and no per-transaction fees.
8. **Quarter-end and year-end checklists tied to deadlines.** Reminders exist; add "what is missing for this quarter" (uncategorised transactions, unmatched invoices, missing receipts). Reason: deadline stress is a named complaint; this turns it into a to-do list.
9. **Accountant handover export.** One-click export of a year's journal, bank transactions, invoices and receipts in a format accountants accept (CSV plus PDFs). Reason: free accountant access is a selling point (MoneyMonk); a self-hosted export removes lock-in.
10. **Data ownership and no per-invoice fees.** Explicitly market against Rompslomp's Nov 2026 per-invoice charge and Moneybird's price changes. Reason: two named market grievances from late 2026.

### Nice-to-have
11. **UBL 2.1 invoice export (NLCIUS / Peppol BIS 3.0 format)** without transmission. Reason: aligns with the 2030 domestic B2B direction and Belgian mandate; transmission can wait.
12. **Multi-currency invoicing with ECB rates.** Reason: "limited" at several vendors; niche for most ZZP'ers.
13. **Payment links via the user's own PSP (Mollie/Stripe) or a static iDEAL QR.** Reason: common in competitor feature lists; a self-hosted app must avoid holding funds.
14. **Dashboards and a "what do I owe in tax this year" card** (zelfstandigenaftrek, MKB-winstvrijstelling, KIA). Reason: the existing IB overview covers this; a visual summary helps users who fear the Belastingdienst.
15. **Shared mobile-first receipt inbox via share sheet** (Android share target for PDF and photo). Reason: a clear mobile UX pattern; not verified for any Dutch competitor, so could be a differentiator.

### Not recommended now
- Full Digipoort/SBR filing: certification burden.
- Peppol transmission: needs an access point.
- Direct PSD2 integration with a licensed provider: needs a licence or a paid aggregator.
- Accountant marketplace or Tellow-style services: outside scope.
